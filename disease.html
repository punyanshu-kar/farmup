Common Regional Crop Diseases

Paddy / Rice
Sheath Blight (Rhizoctonia)

Greenish-grey oval spots on leaf sheaths near water line. Avoid excess urea; spray Hexaconazole 5% EC.

Wheat
Loose Smut (Ustilago tritici)

Ears turn into black powdery mass of spores at heading stage. Treat seeds with Vitavax or Carbendazim before sowing.

Potato
Early Blight (Alternaria solani)

Concentric ring target spots on older foliage. Apply Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2g/L water at first notice.
