FarmUp is an intuitive, field-tested digital agriculture platform designed specifically for Indian farmers. It translates complex market rates, satellite weather data, agronomic research, and government policies into simple, actionable guidance in regional languages.

---

## Key Features & How They Help Farmers

### 1. Today's Mandi Rates (Mandi Bhav)
* **What it is**: Real-time modal spot prices across APMC mandis nationwide, covering all 36 States and Union Territories.
* **How it helps the farmer**:
  * **Price Transparency**: Eliminates middlemen exploitation by showing statutory Minimum Support Prices (MSP) vs. actual open-market rates.
  * **Inter-District Arbitrage**: Allows farmers to compare nearby mandi prices to decide where to sell their harvest for the highest profit.
  * **Quick Search & Filters**: Instant filtering by state, district, and crop variety (Paddy, Wheat, Mustard, Cotton, Potato, Tomato, Soybean, etc.).

---

### 2. Hyper-Local Agricultural Weather & Spray Windows
* **What it is**: Farm-level meteorological forecasts including temperature, rain likelihood, wind velocity, humidity, and soil moisture.
* **How it helps the farmer**:
  * **Pesticide Spray Windows**: Recommends optimal hours for spraying fertilizers and pesticides based on wind speed and dry periods, preventing chemical wastage.
  * **Rain & Frost Alerts**: Provides 7-day rainfall forecasts and hourly precipitation chances so farmers can plan sowing, harvesting, and irrigation safely.
  * **Root-Zone Moisture Tracking**: Helps avoid over-irrigation or water stress during critical crop growth stages.

---

### 3. Crop Doctor (Leaf Disease Diagnosis)
* **What it is**: Visual AI crop scanner that detects plant diseases, fungal infections, bacterial blights, and nutrient deficiencies from a photo of a crop leaf.
* **How it helps the farmer**:
  * **Instant Identification**: Diagnoses common crop diseases across Paddy, Wheat, Mustard, Potato, Cotton, Vegetables, and Pulses in seconds.
  * **Dual Remedy System**: Delivers actionable solutions—both cost-effective organic treatments (Neem oil, Jeevamrut, Trichoderma) and standard chemical fungicides/pesticides with precise dosage guidelines.
  * **Safety Waiting Periods**: Alerts farmers on harvest waiting intervals after chemical application to ensure food safety.

---

### 4. Government Agricultural Schemes & Subsidy Calculator
* **What it is**: A centralized directory and calculation portal for Central and State agricultural welfare schemes.
* **How it helps the farmer**:
  * **PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi**: Direct status tracking for annual direct income support.
  * **PMFBY Crop Insurance Calculator**: Calculates exact farmer premium shares (1.5% Rabi, 2% Kharif, 5% Commercial) and estimated claim sums.
  * **PM-KUSUM & Solar Pumps**: Information on 60%–90% government subsidies for solar agricultural pumps.
  * **Kisan Credit Card (KCC)**: Eligibility rules and documentation needed for low-interest (4% effective) crop production loans.

---

### 5. Predictive Distress Scorer & Field Telemetry
* **What it is**: An automated risk evaluation engine that continuously monitors environmental, market, financial, and structural farm indicators.
* **How it helps the farmer**:
  * **Early Warning System**: Evaluates rainfall deficits, price drops below MSP, debt-to-acreage ratio, and single-crop vulnerability.
  * **Objective Vulnerability Index**: Generates a 0–100% resilience score with immediate agronomic recommendations to protect family cashflow before losses become critical.
  * **KVK Intervention**: Connects distressed farmers directly to Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) district agronomists for expedited assistance.

---

### 6. Kisan Helpline Directory
* **What it is**: A dedicated directory of verified, direct toll-free emergency and advisory hotlines across India.
* **How it helps the farmer**:
  * **1-Tap Direct Calling**: Instant dial links to Kisan Call Centre (1800-180-1551), PM-Kisan Support, PMFBY claim helplines, and State Agriculture Directorates.
  * **Filter by Issue**: Filter contacts specifically for pest advisories, subsidy delays, loan relief, or cyclone/flood emergencies.

---

### 7. Personalized Farmer Dashboard
* **What it is**: A secure portal linked to the farmer's registered land holding, soil type, and crop portfolio.
* **How it helps the farmer**:
  * **Daily Field Directives**: Step-by-step agronomic tasks tailored to current weather and crop age.
  * **Soil & Irrigation Context**: Recommendations aligned with the farmer's actual soil type (Alluvial, Black, Red) and water sources (Canal, Borewell, Drip).
  * **Digital Kisan Pass**: Verifiable farmer credentials for faster access to market yards and banking services.

---

### 8. Multilingual Regional Accessibility
* **What it is**: Complete platform localization supporting 11 major Indian languages:
  * English · हिन्दी (Hindi) · ਪੰਜਾਬੀ (Punjabi) · বাংলা (Bengali) · मराठी (Marathi) · తెలుగు (Telugu) · தமிழ் (Tamil) · ગુજરાતી (Gujarati) · ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada) · മലയാളം (Malayalam) · ଓଡ଼ିଆ (Odia)
* **How it helps the farmer**:
  * Allows farmers across all states to read market rates, weather advisories, and diagnostic guidance in their native mother tongue.
  * Seamlessly switchable both from the desktop top bar and directly inside the mobile menu drawer.

---

## Mobile-First Design
FarmUp is engineered to work smoothly on entry-level smartphones and low-bandwidth 2G/3G rural networks with touch-friendly navigation and zero clutter.
