Government Agricultural Schemes

Direct Benefit Transfer ₹6,000 / yr
PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi

Income support of ₹6,000 per year in 3 equal four-monthly installments of ₹2,000 directly into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts of all landholding farmers.

  • 100% centrally sponsored with zero broker fees.
  • Requires OTP-based or biometric e-KYC verification.
  • 17th installment release status tracker active.

Comprehensive Coverage 1.5% – 2% Rate
PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Complete financial support to farmers suffering crop loss/damage arising out of unforeseen weather events, localized hail, drought, and post-harvest cyclone damage.

  • Only 2% farmer premium for Kharif crops (Paddy, Cotton).
  • Only 1.5% premium for Rabi crops (Wheat, Mustard).
  • Balance 80-90% premium paid equally by Central & State Govt.
Claim within 72h of loss Calculate Premium →
Renewable Energy Up to 60-90% Off
PM-KUSUM (Solar Pumps)

Installation of standalone solar-powered agriculture pumps (3 HP to 7.5 HP) and solarization of existing grid-connected agriculture pumps to cut diesel costs.

  • 60% subsidy (30% Central + 30% State Govt).
  • 30% bank loan available; farmer pays only 10% upfront.
  • Surplus solar energy can be sold back to state DISCOM.

Subsidized Credit 4% Effective Interest
Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Timely credit availability for farmers for cultivation costs, seed/fertilizer inputs, animal husbandry, and fisheries without mortgaging family gold.

  • Collateral-free loan limit up to ₹1.60 Lakh.
  • Interest subvention of 2% + 3% prompt repayment incentive.
  • Flexible ATM-enabled RuPay debit card for input purchases.

Documents Required for Scheme Registration

01
Aadhaar Card

Must be linked with active mobile number for OTP-based e-KYC authentication.

02
Land Records (RoR)

Khatiyan, 7/12 extract, or Patta showing landholding acreage and survey numbers.

03
Bank Passbook

Aadhaar-seeded savings account with IFSC code for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

04
Sowing Declaration

Self-declaration or local Gram Panchayat certificate of current season's sown crop.
